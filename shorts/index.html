Kişisel video otomasyon aracı

Shorts Factory, geliştiricinin kendi YouTube kanalı için kısa videolar hazırlayan ve yükleyen kişisel bir araçtır. Herkese açık bir hizmet değildir; başka kişilerin hesaplarına erişmez.

Shorts Factory

Personal video automation tool

Shorts Factory is a personal tool that prepares and uploads short videos to the developer's own YouTube channel. It is not a public service and does not access anyone else's accounts.

What it does

It produces short fact, quiz and story videos and uploads them only to the developer's own channel that the developer has authorized. YouTube access is limited to the video upload permission (youtube.upload).
